## Notes (심사 메모) — 아래 영문을 그대로 붙여넣으세요

Dari is a job-search app for foreigners living in South Korea. It aggregates publicly available job postings from major Korean recruitment websites and translates them into the user's language so non-Korean speakers can find work.

HOW TO REVIEW
1. On first launch, choose a language, (optionally) a visa type and region, then reach the Home screen. No account or login is required — all features are available immediately.
2. Home shows a scrollable list of job postings. Tap any card to view details (translated title, description, salary, location, and how to apply).
3. Use the Filter button to narrow results by visa type, region, salary, job category, and more. Use Search to find jobs by keyword.
4. Tap the heart icon to save favorites (stored locally on device).
5. In a job's detail screen, "Move to Apply" opens the original job posting inside an in-app browser so the user can apply on the source website.

CONTENT SOURCE / THIRD-PARTY DISCLAIMER
All job listings are collected from third-party Korean recruitment sites and are shown for information purposes only. Each listing displays its source and a disclaimer advising users to verify details on the original site before applying. Dari does not host or own the job postings and does not process applications itself.

PERMISSIONS
- Notifications: used only to alert users when a new job matching their saved filters is posted. Fully optional; the app works without it.
- App Tracking Transparency (ATT): requested for advertising personalization. The app functions normally if the user declines.
- The app does NOT request location; users select their region manually.

ADVERTISING
The app shows Google AdMob banner and interstitial ads to keep the service free.

Thank you for reviewing Dari.
