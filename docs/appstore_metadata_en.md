## 5. Description (4000자 제한)

Dari helps foreigners living in Korea find jobs — in a language you understand.

We gather job listings from major Korean recruitment sites and translate them into your language, so you can search, compare, and apply without the language barrier.

■ In your language
Job titles, descriptions, locations, and salaries are shown in your own language — no more copy-pasting into a translator.

■ Filter to find the right job
Narrow down listings by the things that matter to you:
• Visa type (E-9, E-7, H-2, F-series, and more)
• Region across Korea
• Salary type (hourly, daily, monthly, annual)
• Job category & employment type
• Required Korean level
• Benefits (housing, meals, insurance, and more)

■ Smart search
Search by keyword with typo tolerance. Find jobs by company name or role, even if your spelling isn't perfect.

■ Salary shown clearly
Pay is formatted the way you expect, in your language — so you always know what a job offers.

■ Save your favorites
Bookmark jobs you're interested in and come back to them anytime.

■ New job alerts
Turn on notifications and get alerted the moment a new job matching your filters is posted. Never miss an opportunity.

■ See how to apply
Each listing shows the available ways to apply — online, phone, message, email, and more — so you know exactly what to do next.

Dari is built specifically for the foreign community in Korea. Whether you're on an E-9 work visa, looking to switch to E-7, or searching for part-time work, Dari brings the right opportunities to you.

Start your job search today — in your language.

* Job listings are provided for information purposes. Please verify details on the original source before applying.
