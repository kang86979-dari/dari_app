Dari - Privacy Policy

Last updated: April 18, 2026

1. Overview

Dari ("we", "our", "the app") is a job search application for foreigners in Korea. We are committed to protecting your privacy. This policy explains what data we collect and how we use it.

2. Information We Collect

2.1 Information you provide

  • Language preference — Your selected language for the app interface
  • Visa type selection — Selected visa types for job filtering
  • Favorites — Job postings you save (stored locally on your device)
  • Search history — Recent search keywords (stored locally on your device)

2.2 Information collected automatically

  • Location data — Only when you grant permission, used solely to show nearby job postings. We do not store or transmit your precise location to our servers.
  • Usage analytics — Anonymous session data including screen views, search queries, and filter usage. This helps us improve the app. No personally identifiable information is collected.
  • Device information — App version, platform (Android/iOS), and language code for analytics purposes.

2.3 Information we do NOT collect

  • Name, email, phone number, or any personal identification
  • No account registration required
  • No login or authentication

3. How We Use Information

  • To display job postings in your preferred language
  • To filter jobs based on your visa type and location
  • To improve app functionality based on anonymous usage patterns

4. Data Storage

Your preferences (language, visa, favorites, search history) are stored locally on your device using SharedPreferences. This data is not transmitted to our servers.

Anonymous analytics events are stored in our database (Supabase) without any personally identifiable information.

5. Third-Party Services

  • Google AdMob — We display advertisements. Google may collect device identifiers for ad personalization. See Google's Privacy Policy.
  • Supabase — Our backend database provider. See Supabase Privacy Policy.

6. External Links

The app contains links to external job posting websites. When you tap "Apply", you will be redirected to the original job site. We are not responsible for the privacy practices of these external sites.

7. Children's Privacy

Dari is not intended for use by children under 13. We do not knowingly collect information from children.

8. Data Deletion

Since all personal data is stored locally on your device, you can delete all data by uninstalling the app. No server-side personal data exists to delete.

9. Changes to This Policy

We may update this privacy policy from time to time. Changes will be reflected in the "Last updated" date above.
