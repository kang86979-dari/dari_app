## 자세한 설명

🇰🇷 Dari — The easiest way for foreigners to find jobs in Korea

Dari collects job postings from major Korean job sites and translates them into your language. No more struggling with Korean-only job boards.

✅ Key Features

• Multi-language support — Browse jobs in 17 languages including Chinese, Vietnamese, Thai, Uzbek, Khmer, Nepali, Filipino, Indonesian, Myanmar, Mongolian, Japanese, Sinhala, Bengali, Russian, Hindi, Korean, and English

• Smart filters — Filter by visa type, region, job category, salary, work schedule, Korean level, and more

• Visa-based search — Find jobs that match your visa type (E-9, H-2, F-4, etc.)

• Real-time updates — New job postings added daily from multiple Korean job sites

• Favorites — Save jobs you're interested in and track deadlines

• Fuzzy search — Find jobs even with typos or partial keywords

• Location-based — Find jobs near you with GPS

🔍 Supported Job Sites
Jobs are collected from Alba Heaven, WorkNet, and other major Korean recruitment platforms.

🌏 Who is Dari for?
• Foreign workers in Korea looking for jobs
• International students seeking part-time or full-time work
• Anyone with a Korean work visa

📱 Simple & Fast
• Clean, intuitive interface
• Fast loading with infinite scroll
• Works offline with cached data

Dari means "bridge" — connecting foreigners to opportunities in Korea.
